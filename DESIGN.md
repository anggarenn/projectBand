# Design Direction: Silly N Valley Official Website

## Product Identity
Official web profile, discography catalog, and direct-to-fan merchandise storefront for **Silly N Valley** (`sillynvalley.web.id`). Built for fast mobile/desktop loading, raw zine-inspired print textures, seamless background audio streaming, and direct WhatsApp ordering.

## Dial
Dial: ENERGY 4 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2

## Personality & Voice
- **Tone**: Direct, concise, independent studio/band aesthetic. Zero bloated copywriting or marketing cliches.
- **Hard Gate R-02**: Never use em dashes in UI copy, metadata, comments, or code.

## Color Palette
- **Base Canvas**: Matte Black (`#0A0A0A`)
- **Merch & Modal Surface**: Charcoal (`#282828`) & Studio Panel Grey (`#D9D9D9`)
- **Hero Paper Surface**: Raw Textured Grey (`#C8CACD`)
- **Primary Ink**: Off-White (`#F3EFE0` / `#FFFFFF`)
- **Accent**: Acid Lime (`#D4E627`)

## Typography
- **Display & Headings**: `Bebas Neue` (`--font-bebas`)
- **Body & UI**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`--font-sans`)
